## hook
Claude Ship: you think 1 PR must cost you 4 stops, but AI actually tries the fixes and approves it, if your policy allows.

## before
Here's how. Before, an AI review only left comments. You stopped your next task for a failing check, a merge conflict, a comment, then chased someone to approve.

## new
Now, in beta, PR Steward works on all three while you keep coding, and notes what it changed.

## how
Then Claude Approvals reads your team's policy file, like a harbor rulebook: small routine boats it can stamp itself; risky ones can need people.

## why
Anthropic's Claude Code team, with Claude's review as one layer, more than doubled merged pull requests, June to September.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: in our example, 1 pull request stopped you 4 times; now 0, if the fixes work and your policy allows.
