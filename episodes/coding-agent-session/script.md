## hook
Coding agents, explained: you would never fix your car with the mechanic on the phone, but that is how you use an AI chatbot.

## before
A chat assistant is the mechanic on the phone. He can only talk. So you paste his fix into your code, run it, and carry the error back to him. Again and again.

## new
A coding agent is the mechanic standing in your garage, with tools. You give it one job, in your terminal or your editor. Then it works in a loop. It reads your files. It edits the code. It runs a command, and checks the result. Still broken? It goes around again, until the job is done. That whole visit, every step and every result, is one session.

## why
You stop being the messenger. You watch, and you can stop it at any moment.

## takeaway
It can still be wrong, so give it a test to run. But notice what changed. The mechanic did not get a bigger brain. He got hands, and a way to check his own work. That is what makes it an agent.
