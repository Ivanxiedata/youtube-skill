## hook
Coding agents, 60s: before, you paste your chatbot's code and never stop, but how does 1 agent loop 4 steps until you're done?
Here's how, in one story: a mechanic.

## before
A chatbot is the mechanic on the phone. He can only talk. So you paste his fix, run it, and carry the error back to him.

## new
A coding agent is the same mechanic, in your garage, with tools. You give it one job, in your terminal or editor. It reads your files. It edits the code. It runs a command, and checks the result. Still broken? It goes around again, until the job is done.
That's the answer I promised: one agent, one loop, four steps. Read, edit, run, check. The whole visit is one session.

## why
You stop being the messenger. You watch, and you can stop it at any moment.

## takeaway
It can still be wrong, so give it a test to run. The mechanic did not get a bigger brain. He got hands, and a way to check his own work.
