## hook
Coding agents, 60s: why do you lose hours, never stop pasting your code, but how can 1 agent loop 4 steps until you're done?

## before
A chat assistant is the mechanic on the phone. He can only talk. So you paste his fix, run it, and carry the error back to him. Again and again.

## new
A coding agent is the mechanic in your garage, with tools. You give it one job, in your terminal or your editor. Then it works in a loop. It reads your files. It edits the code. It runs a command, and checks the result. Still broken? It goes around again, until the job is done. That whole visit, every step and every result, is one session.

## why
You stop being the messenger. You watch, and you can stop it at any moment.

## takeaway
It can still be wrong, so give it a test to run. But notice what changed. The mechanic did not get a bigger brain. He got hands, and a way to check his own work. That is what makes it an agent.
