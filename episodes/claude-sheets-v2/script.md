# Script: claude-sheets-v2

## Hook
Claude Sheets, 2026 beta spreadsheet: you think checking AI math will cost you a redo, but actually formulas show how.
Here's how: a math teacher's rule... show your work, so mistakes can show!

## Before
Before, in our example, you pasted Claude's chat budget into another tool as plain text: three totals, no work shown.
Checking meant redoing the math; Claude, outside that tool, couldn't follow your edits.

## New
Now, in the paid-plan beta, it stays in Claude; you both edit the same cells.
Its formulas stay in the cells, like shown work: you can check how each number was calculated.
If our total skipped a quarter, its formula shows it!

## Why
Claude's edits are outlined as they land; a card can undo just those, briefly.

## Takeaway
So, what we learned: our three pasted totals showed no work; in the paid-plan beta, they can be formulas you check.
