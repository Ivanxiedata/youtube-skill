# Script: gpt-6-2-cost-per-task

## Hook
GPT-6.2 Astra model: you pick by price but how much text it uses actually sets your cost.
Here's why: a taxi fare is mostly rate times miles; the route sets the miles.

## Before
Before, you'd compare the rate: price per token, a chunk of text, assuming pricier meant better.

## New
Now Astra's price per token is at least 20 percent below GPT-6 Astra's. Yet on one work test, OpenAI says, it scored higher at under a third of the cost.
Like a driver who picks the best route: OpenAI credits writing fewer tokens, plus lower prices.

## Why
On one coding test, both at max, OpenAI says the cheaper model, Sol, nearly matched Astra's score at about a fifth the cost.

## Takeaway
So, what we learned: price per token is the taxi's rate; your cost is price times tokens: compare cost per task.
