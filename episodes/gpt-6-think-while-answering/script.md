## hook
GPT-6 in ChatGPT: you think smarter answers cost you a longer blank wait, but actually it can start answering before it's done thinking.

## before
Here's how, on one web-search question. Before, it finished thinking first: our 10-second wait... then one block of text.

## new
Now, it answers with what it has so far, keeps thinking, adds parts, sometimes a chart or a calculator: one answer, OpenAI says, just as factual.

## why
OpenAI says GPT-6, on its Instant setting, starts web-search answers 44% sooner, on average, than 5.6 Instant.
So thinking harder needn't delay the start: in OpenAI's internal agent-task test, GPT-6 on Extra High thinking started as soon as 5.6 on Medium.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: our 10-second web-search wait till it starts, on 5.6 Instant, becomes about 6 on GPT-6 Instant, parts still coming.
