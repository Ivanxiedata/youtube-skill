## Student (round 2)

### 1. Main idea
GPT-6 in ChatGPT no longer makes you stare at a blank screen while it thinks hard: it shows a first answer part early and keeps adding to it while it keeps thinking.

### 2. Ideas I learned
**Answering while thinking.** Older thinking models thought silently and then dropped one big block of text. In the video's example, a web-search question meant a 10-second empty wait. GPT-6 answers with what it has so far, keeps thinking, and adds more parts. It is like a robot chef who slides out a first plate while still cooking the next ones.

**Parts can be more than text.** Later parts can be a chart or a calculator, "when it fits your question". All the parts join into one answer, which OpenAI says is just as factual.

**The fast mode starts sooner.** OpenAI says GPT-6 Instant starts web-search answers 44% sooner on average than 5.6 Instant. The video's example went from 10 seconds to about 6.

**Harder thinking doesn't delay the start.** In OpenAI's test on everyday agent tasks, GPT-6 on Extra High thinking started answering as soon as 5.6 on Medium did. So choosing more thinking no longer means a later start.
