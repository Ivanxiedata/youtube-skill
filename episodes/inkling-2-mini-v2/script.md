## hook
Before, you'd waste time watching your AI think out loud; now Inkling 2 Mini thinks more in its head, so you wait less, but how?

## before
Here's how, in one hard exam question. You ask a hard question. An ordinary model, like Gemma or Qwen, thinks by writing: line after line, and you wait for every line.

## new
Same question on Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini, out November 20: it does more of the working in its head, then writes a short note. How? Its middle 12 layers run three times, same weights: 48 layer steps from 24 layers, no bigger brain.

## why
In the maker's test on a research twin, three passes wrote less than half the reasoning. And it's open: about 20 gigabytes, for a 32 gig Mac.

## takeaway
That's how you wait less: less thinking to write out. The catch: each word it writes takes more work, so speed depends on your setup, and it still writes some. Same exam; more of the working happens in its head.
