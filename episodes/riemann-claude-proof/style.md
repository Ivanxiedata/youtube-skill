# Style B: the "news presenter" format (A/B test, 2026-11-24)

The owner asked for a brand-new delivery, learned from one reel, to A/B test against our standard format (style A, `docs/workflow.md`).
Source studied: an Instagram reel by @harryspeaks_ (Mandarin, 2:06, 9:16, 2,488 likes, 21 comments, 2026-11-24) on the same news.
Patterns only: no line, picture or look of the reel is copied; the words are ours and the facts are ours (`research.md`).

## What the reel does
| Device | In the reel |
|---|---|
| Frame | 9:16, one presenter talking straight to camera, chest-up, centred, the whole time |
| Hook | news you missed: "the world moves fast, last week a huge AI step happened and most people missed it" |
| Keyword chip | almost every sentence: one big boxed keyword (`160 YEARS`, `ONE MONTH`, `WHAT DOES IT MEAN?`) above a one-line subtitle |
| Cutaways | every 2-3 sentences a picture for 1-3 s: inset under the chip, or full screen (portrait, manuscript, code file, timeline card, small diagram) |
| Arc | why it's huge -> the shock (how fast / how big) -> "What does this mean?" -> honest "nothing changes for you yet" -> "the real change is next" -> a quick list of everyday wins -> call to action |
| Voice | excited, fast, a rhetorical question at every turn, numbers punched |

## Style B for this episode: copy the reel's style exactly (owner, 2026-11-24)
The owner: "the goal is to replicate the exact style; scoring may not be accurate to reflect the winning style".
So our style-A scorers and rules do NOT judge this episode: no topic-first hook (L14), no hookscore bar, no title card at frame 0 or corner tag,
no turn by 10 s, no 60 s cap, no side-by-side diagram. What still applies: every claim is true (`claims.md`), and the video is technically clean
(no see-through figures, no overlapping subtitles, no glitches, the audit passes, the voice says every word right).

Layout, measured from the reel (1080 x 1920; y as a share of the height):
| Element | Where and how |
|---|---|
| Background | a plain warm beige-grey wall (about #CFC6B8 to #A89F92), a soft darker corner shadow at the top, like a room corner; nothing else |
| Presenter | our stickman, huge and centred like a selfie: head top at about 16%, chin at about 53%, head about 45% of the width; shoulders and hoodie fill the bottom of the frame. One hand comes up beside the face to gesture (counting, pointing, OK sign, open palm) |
| Chip | a pale white-blue box (#E8EEF4), dark navy bold text (#1E2A44), centred at about 60-65%, sized to its text plus padding; one per line, changing at sentence breaks |
| Subtitle | the spoken words, white bold with a dark shadow, no box, centred at about 67-69%, one line (two short lines at most) |
| Inset | a card or drawing at about 70-92%, about two thirds of the width, sharp corners: cream "info cards" (title row, big numbers, a thin red timeline) or simple drawings in our stickman look |
| Full-screen | for the biggest moments only (the 17 MILLION counter, the CERTIFIED stamp, WHAT WE LEARNED): the picture fills the frame, the chip and subtitle stay on top |
| Length | about 90 s: about 235 spoken words in Ivan's voice, at most 5% over |
| Our brand kept | Ivan's voice, the stickman's look (cream head, coral hoodie), the ending: "So, what we learned:" then subscribe and comment (L32) |
