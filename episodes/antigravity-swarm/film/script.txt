Antigravity Swarm: you think three separate files cost you three waits, but actually three agents code them at once, and usually another tests them before you look.
Here's what changed. Before, one agent took users, orders and products in turn; after each, you ran the tests yourself.
Now, in preview, you type slash swarm, and your chat becomes the coordinator: it gives each file its own worker, at the same time, plus, usually, a verifier that runs the tests and sends problems back to the coder.
They all talk on one shared board you can watch: questions, test results.
The catch: it works best when a task splits cleanly, and it uses your quota much faster.
So, what we learned: three files used to mean three waits; now, when the task splits, three agents take them at once, and usually a verifier tests first.
