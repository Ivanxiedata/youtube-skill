## hook
Inkling 2 Mini: before, you'd stop and wait; your AI's written thinking cost you; now 12 layers loop 3 times, but how, why?

## before
Here's how, in one hard exam question. Before, an ordinary model, like Gemma or Qwen, ran each layer once per token. To think harder, it wrote its working out, line after line, and you waited.

## new
Now, since November 20, Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini works it over in its head. Its middle 12 layers run three times, same weights: 48 layer steps from 24 layers, no bigger brain.

## why
So it can write less. In the maker's test on a research twin, three passes wrote less than half the reasoning. And it's open, about 20 gigabytes, for a 32 gig Mac.

## takeaway
How? The middle layers loop. Why? More thinking per token, fewer tokens to wait for. The catch: it still writes some reasoning, and loops add no new facts. Same exam; more working happens in its head.
