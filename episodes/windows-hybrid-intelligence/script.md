## hook
Windows hybrid intelligence: you think sorting 20 files with Copilot will cost you 40 steps, but actually, soon on Copilot+ PCs, you never upload them yourself.

## before
Here's what changes. Today, many Copilot tasks run in the cloud. In our example, twenty files: twenty uploads, twenty moves, forty steps.

## new
Soon, on Copilot+ PCs, with your permission, it reads them on your PC. You ask, say yes, and it sorts them: two steps.
How? Windows picks, task by task: your PC, or the cloud when needed. Supported agents can run in a container limited to allowed files and networks.

## why
Copilot+ PCs already run over two trillion local AI inferences a month.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: our twenty files took forty steps; soon, on Copilot+ PCs, two, with your permission.
