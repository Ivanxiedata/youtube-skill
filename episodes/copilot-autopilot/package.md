## The viewer's moment (lesson L16)
- **Who** (`docs/voice.md`): someone who uses Copilot at work and runs projects with many people in them (a supplier review, a launch); curious, not a machine-learning engineer.
- **The pain today:** a two-week supplier review with 10 follow-ups (our example); in Copilot Chat each follow-up is one more prompt you type, so 10 prompts, and on a day of back-to-back meetings nothing moves (`research.md`, Before and the counted example, which is ours).
- **The moment they're paying for:** you give Autopilot one goal; while you're in meetings the follow-ups go out on their own in Teams and Outlook, and it can check with you before key actions (`research.md` rows 4, 5, 8, 11). Rolling out to Frontier customers (row 1).
