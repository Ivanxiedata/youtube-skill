## hook
ChatGPT Space: you think a team plan costs you nothing, but 12 Slack decisions actually cost you 12 copies by hand, until you set it to update itself.

## before
Here's why. Before, ChatGPT gave you a reply or a file in your Library. Each Slack decision got copied into the plan by hand, like an old scoreboard.

## new
Now Space is rolling out to Plus: the plan is a shared page, set to update from your connected Slack. Twelve decisions, zero copies by hand.

## why
How? Connect your apps, set the page to update, and your team opens the same page. That's OpenAI's own example: a project plan that stays current with decisions from Slack.

## takeaway
The catch: colleagues need Space access, and you still check it before sharing. So, what we learned: twelve decisions used to mean twelve copies by hand; now the page updates itself, once you set it.
