## hook
OpenAI dots: before, your ChatGPT would stop, wait for you; now your dot works 24/7, but how, at what risk, in 60 seconds?

## before
Here's how, in one shop you run. Old ChatGPT, even 2025's agent mode, was the clerk at the counter: you ring, it does that one task, then waits for the next ring. Go home, and no new work starts.

## new
Now a dot works the night shift. It has its own desk, a cloud computer, and your goals pinned on the wall. While you're away, it reads your connected apps, but it can't send a thing.

## why
Morning, a note: you forgot an invoice; it's ready; send it? You tap yes. Say "shorter notes", and it learns. And changing a password always stays with you.

## takeaway
How? Its own computer, working toward your goals. What risk? Away, it only reads; it asks before sensitive steps. It still makes mistakes, so check its work. The shop never closes now, but the keys are still yours.
