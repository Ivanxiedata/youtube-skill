# Script: claude-sheets

## Hook
Claude Sheets, 2026 beta: you think checking AI numbers will cost you a redo, but actually formulas show how.
Here's how, on our hiring budget: three teams, four quarters.

## Before
Before, in our example, you pasted Claude's chat table into another tool: three totals, just text, no formulas.
Checking meant redoing the math; Claude had no live access to your edits.

## New
Now, in the paid-plan beta, the spreadsheet stays in Claude; you both edit the same cells.
Claude's formulas stay in the cells, so you can check how each number was calculated: if our total skipped a quarter, its formula shows it.

## Why
Claude's edits are outlined as they land, so you see them; when it finishes, a card can undo just those edits, but only briefly.

## Takeaway
So, what we learned: pasted, our three totals hid their math; in the paid-plan beta, they're formulas you can check.
