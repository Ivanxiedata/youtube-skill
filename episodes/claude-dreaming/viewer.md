2. "Anthropic's memory cleanup: you never check A I agents' notes,": a desk at dusk. A robot with a teal screen for a head writes in an open notebook labelled "AGENT'S NOTES". A stick figure in a coral hoodie with an amber backpack strap glances at it and walks past. The picture slowly moves closer.
3. "but why can one wrong note cost you?": close on the notebook page: grey handwritten lines; one line turns amber, and a tag "1 WRONG NOTE" points at it.
4. "Here's why: class notes, scribbled fast.": a classroom: a board with chalk lines; the stick figure in the coral hoodie sits at a desk and scribbles fast in a notebook, the pencil jumping.
5. "One wrong line, and the mistake repeats.": the notebook page with one amber line; beside it a test paper where the same amber answer appears, crossed with an amber X; a tag "SAME MISTAKE".
6. "An agent's memory is notes it wrote itself.": a yellow tab "BEFORE". The robot from shot 2 writes in its notebook "AGENT'S NOTES"; a card under it: "MEMORY = NOTES IT WROTE ITSELF".
7. "In Anthropic's Pokemon test,": a simple game map from above: a grey mountain with a cave mouth, a path below it, and a step-like ledge on the path. A tag: "ANTHROPIC'S POKÉMON TEST". The robot stands by the cave with a small note card of scribbled lines and an amber "WRONG NOTE" stamp on it.
8. "Claude kept trusting one wrong note:": the robot reads its note card, nods, walks along the path and hops off the ledge, landing back on the route below; it reads the card again and walks the same way. A meter on the right, titled "DROPS OFF THE LEDGE", ticks from 0 to 1, then 2.
9. "three drops back the way it came.": the robot drops off the ledge again and lands on the route it came from; a tag "BACK THE WAY IT CAME" points at it; the meter ticks to 3 and glows amber.
10. "Good students fix theirs from the recording.": a teal tab "NOW". The stick figure at a desk at home, a laptop playing the class (a play bar moving); the old notebook on the left, and on the right a new notebook where the stick figure writes a clean copy; the amber line becomes a teal line.
11. "Dreaming, a research preview in the Claude Managed Agents service, does that.": a card with a small crescent moon: "DREAMING", under it "RESEARCH PREVIEW", under that "CLAUDE MANAGED AGENTS: A SERVICE THAT RUNS AGENTS".

13. "and writes a clean copy.": an arrow from both into a new notebook labelled "CLEAN COPY"; its lines are neat and the wrong note is now a teal line with a check mark. The notebook "ITS NOTES" stays where it was.
14. "Note fixed,": the game map again, the tag "ANTHROPIC'S POKÉMON TEST" at the top; the robot holds its note card, the amber "WRONG NOTE" stamp gone and a teal check mark on it; the meter "DROPS OFF THE LEDGE" still shows 3 from before.
15. "Claude reached the next city, Anthropic says.": the robot walks into the cave mouth and comes out on the far side, where small houses stand; a teal stamp "REACHED THE NEXT CITY". The meter does not move.
