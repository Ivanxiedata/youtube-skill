## hook
Claude computer use: you think 20 orders in an app with no connector cost you 20 copy-pastes, but actually Claude can type them, almost alone.

## before
Here's how. Before, on Team and Enterprise, Claude couldn't click or type in that app. So you copied every order into the chat, and typed the answers back yourself. 20 orders, 20 round trips.

## new
Now, in beta on Team and Enterprise, once an owner turns it on, Claude takes screenshots to see your screen, then clicks and types in the apps you allow. You approve 2 apps. Claude does the 20. How does it choose? In Cowork, the most precise tool first: a connector, then the browser, and your screen last.

## why
Anthropic's own catch: screen work is slower than a connector, and complex tasks sometimes need a second try.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: 20 orders used to mean 20 copy-pastes; now Claude types them, and you approve 2 apps.
