## hook
Prompt caching: you think your 100-page PDF costs you once, but without it, 30 questions actually cost you full price 30 times.

## before
Here's why. Without caching, it's a student re-reading the whole book per question. Question one: all 100 pages, full price. Question two: all again. By question thirty: paid thirty times.

## new
With prompt caching, question one pays a quarter more, by default, to save its work. Questions two to thirty reuse that work for about a tenth on most models. How? The document goes first, the same every time; only the new question is read fresh.

## why
OpenAI's math: ten requests cost just over two full reads, not ten. Ours for thirty: about four.

## takeaway
The catch: on Claude, unused saved work can fade after five minutes, by default. So, what we learned: keep the document first, and it's fully read once, not thirty times.
