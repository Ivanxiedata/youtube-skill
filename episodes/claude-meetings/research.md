## Before (the predecessor)
The maker's line implies it: "Claude writes the notes and action items, so no one on the call has to" [Blog], i.e. before, someone on the call had to.
The scene the viewer has lived: a call where someone takes notes, a question gets "I'll check and get back to you", and after the call someone still writes up the plan and the follow-ups.
Note-taker bots (the planner's "before") record and send a summary afterwards; the maker itself names note-taker tools only as partners
("We're working with partners like Gong, Attention, Granola, Fireflies.ai, Otter, and Plaud" [Blog]) and calls one "another company's notetaker bot" [Use].

**Narrowing, so the video does not overclaim:** today's note-taker products also do live things (Fireflies: "Fred gives answers, suggestions, and coaching in real time, right inside the call",
https://fireflies.ai/; Otter: "coaches you live based on what's been said", https://otter.ai/). So the video never says other tools can't do this and names no other product (`docs/voice.md`: never claim one tool beats another).
The "before" is the viewer's scene (notes, lookups and the plan wait until after the call), not a claim about a competitor.

## Left out, and why
- Partners (Gong, Otter, ...), auto-join settings, admin roles, ZDR/HIPAA/CMEK, the 8-hour limit: true but not the viewer's moment; in the description where useful.
- Slides: in [Notes] and [Use] but not in the launch article; the video says doc, whiteboard, notes, action items.
- Pricing details: only "free in beta" for notes; the connected-tool usage caveat goes in the description.
- No speed or time-saved number: the maker gives none.

## Where the analogy stops
The story is the maker's own example, an event-planning call (a team plans a party/event). Claude is drawn as a screen-head robot in the call grid (a visible participant).
It asks before looking up (drawn as an "OK?" bubble the teammate answers). It does not run the meeting or decide anything: people ask, it writes.
It is a beta, on Team and Enterprise, on Google Meet only for now: said out loud.
