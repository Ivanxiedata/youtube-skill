- **Hook (0.0-9.3 s):** the sentence above; title card from frame 0; single player -> the shared Workspace; "1 GOAL".
- **Turn (9.8 s):** "Here's how." Then it does it, on our 4-part team plan.
- **Before, the pain as a scene (10.9-19.3 s):** four people, each alone with a private chat; each answer pasted into the plan by hand; the meter to 4.
- **Now, the same scene (19.8-27.2 s):** one shared Workspace (early access); an agent drafts a part in the plan; you review; a comment; an agent picks it up; the meter stays at 0.
