## hook
Copilot Workspaces: you think AI teamwork must cost you copy-pastes, but in early access you and agents actually share 1 space, 1 goal.

## before
Here's how. Our example: a 4-part team plan. Before, each of you asked Copilot alone, then pasted its answer in by hand. 4 copy-pastes.

## new
Now, in an early-access Workspace, an agent drafts a part in the plan, and you review. You leave a comment; an agent picks it up.

## why
Microsoft says AI has mostly been single player. Workspaces makes it multiplayer: people and agents, 1 goal, shared context. It grew out of Copilot Notebooks, continues in Teams, and is in Frontier, Microsoft's early access.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: in our example, 4 solo chats meant 4 copy-pastes; in 1 early-access Workspace, agents draft and you review.
