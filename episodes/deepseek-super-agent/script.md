## hook
Napoleon led more than a hundred thousand soldiers. He could not shout orders at each one.

## before
Today, you are the general. Your soldiers are AI coding agents. You still shout at each one, step by step. DeepSeek's team says one person can follow only three to five.

## new
Napoleon's fix was one man: Berthier, his chief of staff. Napoleon said what he wanted. Berthier wrote the orders and sent them to the corps: small armies that could each fight alone. DeepSeek's Super-agent works the same way. One router agent listens on GitHub and in your team chat. Ask once. It opens a ticket and hands it to a worker agent, with its own session and its own folder. Many workers march at once.

## why
The router stays quiet. It speaks up only when you must decide, or can use a result.

## takeaway
It is still a research prototype. But the lesson is two hundred years old. The limit was never the soldiers. It was the general's attention. And now, that general is you.
