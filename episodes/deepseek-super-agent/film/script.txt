You can only babysit five AI agents before you are the bottleneck. That estimate comes from DeepSeek's Super-agent team.
You are a general, and your soldiers are AI agents. Napoleon had the same problem. He could not shout orders at a hundred thousand soldiers.

Napoleon's fix was one man: Bairtyay, his chief of staff. Napoleon said what he wanted. Bairtyay wrote the orders and sent them to the corps: small armies that could each fight alone. DeepSeek's Super-agent works the same way. One router agent listens on GitHub and in your team chat. Ask once. It opens a ticket and hands it to a worker agent, with its own session and its own folder. Many workers march at once.
The router stays quiet. It speaks up only when you must decide, or can use a result.
It is still a research prototype. But the lesson is two hundred years old. The limit was never the soldiers. It was the general's attention. And now, that general is you.
