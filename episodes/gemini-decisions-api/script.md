## hook
Gemini Decisions API: you think 20 questions about 1 insurance claim actually cost you 20 prompts, but 1 call picks all 20 answers, each with how sure it is.

## before
Here's how. Before, you asked a chat model one at a time: the claim sent 20 times, 20 essay answers, no number for how sure.

## new
Now your app sends the claim once with all 20 questions: yes or no, multiple choice, a score. Like one answer sheet.

## how
It doesn't write; it scores the options you gave it. Each answer gets a confidence, 0 to 1; unsure ones can go to a person.

## why
Up to 1,000 questions per call, the claim sent once. Google's own benchmarks: nearly 5 times cheaper than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: in our example, 20 questions took 20 prompts; now 1 call, each answer with how sure it is.
