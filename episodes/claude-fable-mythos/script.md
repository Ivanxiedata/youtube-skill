## hook
Claude Fable 5.2: you think safety must cost you a weaker model, but Anthropic says it's actually the same underlying model as Mythos 5.2.

## before
Here's how: our example, 10 requests. Before, with no checker strong enough, Mythos Preview went to a small group of cyber defenders and infrastructure providers. You got a weaker one.

## new
Now, a separate AI checker reads our requests first. 9 pass to the full model. 1 asks for attack code... blocked! In Claude apps, an older Opus model usually answers, and you're told.

## why
In Anthropic's early tests, 5.2's checker blocked about 55% fewer requests than 5.1's. So fewer requests leave the full model.

## takeaway
So, what we learned: in our example, all 10 once got a weaker model. Now 9 reach the full model; in Claude apps, the checker usually sends the blocked one to older Opus.
