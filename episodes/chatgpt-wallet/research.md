# Research: ChatGPT Wallet (2026-11-13) vs typing your card into ChatGPT agent's browser (takeover mode)

Done 2026-11-22 (daily run, workflow step 1). The maker's own pages first: OpenAI's launch post for ChatGPT Wallet, the 2025 ChatGPT agent post
and the ChatGPT agent help article. Press (Help Net Security) only for what OpenAI does not say. Two items (before, new), so the pages were read directly

## Premise check
**Holds.** OpenAI's own post (dated November 13, 2026) says ChatGPT Wallet is "the first wallet that lets agents pay without access to users' actual card details";
"before each purchase, the user reviews the items, total, and selected card" and "approves it with a security check such as Face ID or a fingerprint scan";
"after the user approves, the payment network provides a payment token—a substitute for the user's actual card details—with a card number, security code, and expiration date";
the charge goes to the user's selected card "without ChatGPT Wallet sharing their actual card details with the business or agent". It launches "in dots".
The "before" holds in OpenAI's own ChatGPT agent pages: the agent asks permission "before taking actions with real-world consequences, like making a purchase",
and the help article says "use takeover mode for sensitive inputs" (you take over the agent's virtual browser and type yourself; screenshots are not captured).

Two narrowings of the topic's wording, so the video does not overclaim:
- The topic's "nothing stood between the agent and the real card" is not said. In takeover mode the agent does not capture what you type, so the video does not say the agent saw your card. It says what the sources support: the agent stopped, you took over and typed your real card, and the shop got your real card number, code and expiry (that is what typing a card into a checkout form gives the shop).
- OpenAI's "payment network" is said as "the card network" (Visa, Mastercard, American Express are the partners named); the video names no network.

## The story: the errand (everyday life)
Everyone has sent someone to the corner shop for them. The question is what you hand them to pay with.
| Story | Product | Holds because |
|---|---|---|
| You send a helper to the shop | an agent shopping for you | agent and dots buy on your behalf |
| Before: the helper (screen-head robot) stops at the till and waves you over; you come and type your own card into the till; the till's screen shows all three numbers | ChatGPT agent asks before a purchase; you take over its browser for sensitive inputs; the shop's form receives your real card | agent post, help article |
| Now: the helper is a teal dot that carries a wallet | Wallet runs in dots | Wallet post |
| At the till it shows you the items, the total and the card on your phone; you press your thumb or look at it | review, then Face ID or fingerprint | Wallet post |
| The card network's booth hands the helper a teal stand-in card with its own number, code and date | the payment token | Wallet post, Help Net Security |
| The till charges the stand-in; the bill lands on your real card at home, with its rewards | charge posts to the real card; rewards and dispute protections kept | Wallet post |
| Your real card stays in your pocket at home; neither the helper nor the till ever sees it | not shared with the business or agent | Wallet post |
| A shop with a gate that stops the helper | some sites may block Wallet purchases | Wallet post |

Where the analogy stops (not said in the video):
- The ChatGPT agent help article (checked by the judge on 2026-11-22) now says ChatGPT agent "is no longer available"; the video speaks of it in the past tense ("Before, ChatGPT's agent stopped at checkout"), so it stays accurate. The permission-before-purchase quote is from the 2025 agent post, not the help article.
- In takeover mode the agent does not capture what you type (help article), so the "before" helper is not shown reading your card; the point is that you had to step in, and the shop got your real card.
- Someone still holds your real card: you add it to ChatGPT Wallet once, and the card network maps the token to it. "Never sees" is about the agent and the business, as OpenAI says.
- OpenAI does not say the token is single-use or tied to one shop; the video says only "a stand-in" per approval. (Its 2025 Instant Checkout tokens were "only authorized for specific amounts and specific merchants"; that is a different product.)
- Earlier ChatGPT paying paths existed: Instant Checkout (September 29, 2025, Etsy sellers and Stripe tokens, [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/)) only at participating merchants; press reports Visa's June 2026 ChatGPT announcement ([ADN/AP](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/2026/06/10/visa-plugs-its-payment-network-into-chatgpt-letting-ai-agents-shop-and-pay-for-users/)). The video's "before" is the agent shopping on an ordinary website, which is what Wallet replaces; it does not say agents could never pay before.
- Approval is per purchase today; letting the agent buy within limits without asking is OpenAI's plan, not a feature.
