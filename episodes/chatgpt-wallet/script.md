## hook
ChatGPT Wallet: before, you'd stop, type your card; now 1 scan, it pays, never seeing your 3 numbers, but how, why, in 60 seconds?

## before
Here's how, in one errand: you send a helper to the shop. Before, ChatGPT's agent stopped at checkout. You took over its browser, typed your real card, and the shop got all three numbers.

## new
Now, since November 13, your dot carries a wallet. At checkout it shows you the items, the total and the card. You approve with Face ID or a fingerprint. Then the card network hands over a stand-in: its own number, code and date.

## why
The shop charges the stand-in; the bill lands on your real card, rewards and all. Your agent never sees your card, and neither does the shop.

## takeaway
How? One scan, then a stand-in card. Why? Your three numbers stay out of the errand. For now, only Pro users in America, and some shops may block it. Your helper still runs the errand; it just never holds your wallet.
