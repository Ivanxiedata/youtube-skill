# feedback

Turns owner comments, evaluations and YouTube numbers into lessons the next episode must follow.

Spec: `docs/specs/feedback.md` · Progress: `feedback/progress.md` (update before you stop) · Check: `make check-feedback`

## Notes
- Record the owner's feedback in their words, in the same session it was given.
- Studies of other channels record patterns only (length, pacing, structure, packaging). Never copy a premise, line, character or look.
- A lesson needs a rule: what to do from now on. "Be better" is not a rule.
